Check that your references are real.

Paste a reference list or load your Word manuscript. Citation Verifier looks up every reference in Crossref, OpenAlex and PubMed, then tells you which ones match, which need a second look, and which it cannot find.

What it checks

Each reference is compared with a real record, not just looked up.

The identifier
It looks up the DOI or PubMed ID and compares the title, first author and year with what you wrote.
The real paper
If the DOI points to a different paper, or there is no DOI, it searches for the closest real match.
What differs
Changed title words and swapped author order are pointed out on the card, so you see the difference and not just a score.
Retractions
Papers marked as retracted in OpenAlex or Crossref are labelled.
Your Word file
Load a .docx manuscript and it fills in the reference list for you to read through before checking.

How to use it

  1. DownloadGet CitationVerifier.exe with the button above.
  2. Open itDouble-click the file. A black window opens, then a page in your browser. Keep the window open while you work and close it to quit.
  3. CheckPaste your references or load a .docx file, click Check references, and read the note on every card. Results can be saved as a CSV.

What the results mean

✓

MatchTitle, first author and year agree with a real record. Read any note that comes with it.

?

ReviewSomething differs, or the identifier points to a different paper. Look at it yourself. It does not mean the reference is fake.

✕

MismatchThe identifier is real but belongs to a paper with a different title, and no other real paper fits.

✕

Not foundNo identifier or search result matches. Some books, theses and old papers are missing from these databases, so check by hand.

What it cannot do

Please read this before relying on it.

  • It does not check that a paper supports the sentence you cite it for. Only reading the paper can do that.
  • A single changed word in a long title can still be marked Match. The note on the card shows the difference, so read the notes.
  • Author order is checked for the first six authors only, and only for authors that appear in your reference.
  • English-language references only. Other languages have not been tried.
  • The retraction check relies on OpenAlex and Crossref and may be incomplete.
  • It has been tried on a small number of real reference lists. There is no measured accuracy figure yet.
  • Up to 200 references per run, and Word files must be .docx (not .doc or PDF).
  • It does not update itself. Download new versions from the releases page.

Privacy

Your references, and your email address if you type one in, are sent to Crossref, OpenAlex, PubMed (NCBI) and doi.org so they can be looked up. The tool stores nothing and there is no account. Word files are read on your own computer.

Do not use it on reference lists that must stay confidential.
